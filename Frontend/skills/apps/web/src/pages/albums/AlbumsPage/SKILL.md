name: web-albums-page
description: "Gallery album listing and management on the web."
---

Gallery opens Albums. The shared album date view shows only dates through today in the displayed month. Selecting a date filters loaded albums by creation day. The fixed carousel shows the current album in a raised frame and the next album in a flat compact row. Move Up or an upward swipe or drag advances one album. A downward gesture reverses the change. Browser Back from an album restores its date and position. Empty dates use the shared empty state.

New album appears in the empty state. Creating an album or activating the main card's Send Horizontal link opens its detail. Titles and owner labels are static. Owners edit or delete an album from its detail page. Creation invalidates album queries.

Legacy Favorites URLs redirect to the album list.
