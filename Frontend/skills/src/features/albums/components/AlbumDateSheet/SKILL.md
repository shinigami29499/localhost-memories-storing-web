---
name: album-date-sheet
description: "Browse album history and select one date from the album calendar sheet."
---

`Frontend/src/features/albums/components/AlbumDateSheet.tsx` opens a native modal from the shared album date bar. The calendar is laid out before the entrance animation starts. The sheet fills in album data from remaining feed pages, enables Done when loading finishes, and offers Retry on failure. Loading uses the page's shared query and existing permissions. The bottom sheet fades in the backdrop, then eases the panel up into place. Dismissal slides the panel down, then fades the backdrop. Interface motion and reduced-motion settings control both.

The calendar shows one month. Swiping left or right on its day grid changes the displayed month within the available range without changing the selected draft. Years run from 2020 through the current year. Each year has all 12 months, including months without albums and future months of the current year. Users can confirm dates from January 1, 2020 through today. Occupied years and months show a dot and accessible label. The three heat levels represent one, two, and three or more albums. Light mode goes from Almond Cream through Taupe to Dark Coffee. Dark mode reverses the order. Accessible day labels include the full album count.

The selected date is a draft until Done. A blue, violet, and pink outline marks selection. It rotates when interface motion is enabled and reduced motion is off. Selected dates and today use a near-black fill with white text in light mode and a white fill with dark text in dark mode. Other dates show their album count through the heat fill. A translucent white hover tint blends with the existing fill. Taupe uses white day text. Month precedes Year. Labels float over the top borders of their controls. Closed selectors use inset shadows. Open selectors and selected options use raised shadows with contrasting monochrome fills. The popup centers its selected month or year when the scroll range allows it. Choosing another day replaces the draft. Escape, outside click, and a down swipe dismiss the draft. The bar restores focus to the month trigger after closing. Done is a borderless pill that matches the mobile navigation size and uses inverted monochrome colors by theme. `Frontend/tests/e2e/album-date-view.spec.ts` covers pagination, selector bounds, heat levels, selection confirmation, and dismissal.
