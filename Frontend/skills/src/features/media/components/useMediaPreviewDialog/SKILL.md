---
name: use-media-preview-dialog
description: "Browse and act on active media in the viewer."
---

The web and mobile media viewers use this hook for carousel state, focus, favorites, album saving, image copying, and map navigation. Album media is deleted through album selection controls.

Mobile album detail uses the immersive option. It renders one full-width slide at a time and disables the membership lookup while the album-saving control is absent. Its drag guard gives zoom, pan, and video controls precedence. A swipe can start on the immersive video surface outside those controls.
