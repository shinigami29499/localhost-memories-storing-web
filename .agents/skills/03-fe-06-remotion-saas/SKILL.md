Remotion can power simple render forms, video editors, and embedded previews. Use the `<Player>` to embed a Remotion preview in a React app. Choose client-side or server-side rendering based on the product requirements, including Lambda, Vercel, Cloudflare, and Node.js options.

Read current guides:

- Framework selection: https://www.remotion.dev/docs/using-a-framework
- Player: https://www.remotion.dev/docs/player
- Rendering: https://www.remotion.dev/docs/renderer
- Vue: https://www.remotion.dev/docs/vue
- Angular: https://www.remotion.dev/docs/angular
- Svelte: https://www.remotion.dev/docs/svelte
